Why four pages, not one CV

Most of the work I do doesn't sit under one job title. In the same month I might be reconciling a farm's operational report, shooting a brand campaign, and flying a mapping mission over a field I helped manage the week before. Rather than flatten that into a single résumé, this site keeps each discipline on its own page — with its own colour marker in the nav — so you can go straight to the part of the work you came for.
